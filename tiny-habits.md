### Question 7
Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit harder to do?

To avoid bad habits
Increasing friction: Add more steps between you and the bad behaviour
Invisible cues: Make the steps for bad habits harder to see or encounter 
Unattractive and hard: Change your environment to make the bad habit feel more difficult.
