Learning process answers 

### Question 1
What is the Feynman Technique? Explain in 1 line.

The Feynman Technique is a learning method where you understand a concept by explaining it in simple words as if you were teaching it to someone else.

### Question 3
What are active and diffused modes of thinking?

Focused : You turn your attention to solve a problem or learn new concept.
Diffused : Relaxed state where we come up with new ideas, we refine those & add those to solve problems.  
### Question 4
According to the video, what are the steps to take when approaching a new topic? Only mention the points.
1. Deconstruct the skill: Decide what you want to learn exactly. Break the skill into smaller pieces.
2. Learn enough to self-correct: get three to four resources about what it is you are trying to learn.
3. Remove Practice barriers:  Remove Distractions around you like telivision or phone.
4. Practice at least 20 hours about topic.
