 Listening and Assertive Communication Answers

 ### Question 1
 What are the steps/strategies to do Active Listening? 
 1. Avoid distracting from your own thoughts.
 2. keep your attention on the speaker and to the topic.
 3. Avoid interrupting the speaker, wait until they have finished. 
 4. Use phrases that say you are interested in that topic.
 5. Use appropriate body language to show that you are engaged in the listening.
 6. Take notes during important discussions.      

 ### Quesion 2
 According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening?
1. Listen Carefully: Pay full attention to what the other person is saying without interrupting them.

2. Understand Their Emotions: Try to understand not only their words but also the emotions and feelings behind them.

3. Rephrase in Your Own Words: Repeat the speaker's message using your own simple words instead of copying their exact sentences.

4. Confirm Your Understanding: Ask the speaker whether your understanding is correct or not.

5. Show Empathy: Make the speaker feel that you genuinely care about their thoughts.
6. Avoid Judging: Do not give advice or make assumptions immediately.
